A function $f$ and its derivative $f'$ have domain $(-\infty,\infty)$.

Some values are given below:

\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
x & -1 & 0 & 1 & 2 \\
\hline
f(x) & -12 & -9 & -7 & -5 \\
f'(x) & 5 & 3 & \frac{3}{2} & \frac{1}{2}
\end{array}
\]

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Find the linear approximation $L(x)$ for $f$ at $x=1$.
  \item Use $L(x)$ to estimate $f(1.3)$.
  \item Explain whether your estimate is an overestimate or an underestimate.
\end{enumerate}
